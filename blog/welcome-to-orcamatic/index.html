Orcamatic makes privacy-first tools for the web. ORCA, our browser automation agent, is the first one.

August 1, 2026

Orcamatic makes tools for the web, with privacy in mind. This site is the home of ORCA: a browser agent designed to take the repeatable pieces of online work off your plate without asking you to move everything into a new system.

ORCA, our first product

ORCA is a privacy-first Chrome extension that automates repetitive browser tasks locally. Show it a task once, and it gives you a reusable way to run that task again.

That could mean filling a regular form, updating a CRM record, moving details between browser tools, collecting a list of information, or checking a recurring page. The point is not to replace your judgment. It is to keep you from redoing the clicks that do not need it.

Why start in the browser?

A lot of real work lives in the browser already. It is where your tools, records, dashboards, and conversations are. ORCA starts there too: it lets you point to the fields and actions that matter on the live page, save the workflow, and run it later.

  • Show it once: select the parts of a task you want ORCA to remember.
  • Keep it close: saved profiles and normal workflow data remain in your browser.
  • Use it your way: run a task on demand or set a schedule for work that comes back regularly.
Good automation should save time without making the work feel farther away.

What you can find here

This site is where we explain the product, publish notes about browser automation and privacy, and keep the practical details easy to find. Start with the ORCA product page, compare the plans, or read the privacy policy before you install.
