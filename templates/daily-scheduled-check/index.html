The Monday 08:00 sweep, scheduled

For the check you do at the start of the day whether or not there is anything in it: queue counts, an approval list, a filing status page, a stock or price column. Same steps as any workflow: the difference is that the scheduler starts it.

  • Setup about 4 min
  • 5 mornings a week
  • Runs in your browser

The steps, written out

Build them in the ORCA panel. This is a recipe rather than a file to load: one sitting, then it is saved as your own profile.

  1. Build the check as a normal workflow

    Navigate, wait for the table, extract the three values you actually look at. If it is useful on demand, it is useful on a timer.

  2. Make it write something down

    A run nobody reads is a rumour. Write the values to a local file, or append them under the previous day so a change is visible at a glance.

  3. Put it on the schedule

    Set the recurring time in the panel. The scheduler runs your saved profile at that time, in your browser profile, on your machine.

  4. Decide the failure behaviour now

    If the page is unreachable: retry every five minutes, or one line in the log? Choose before you are away and someone asks why the file is empty.

  5. Stay in the loop for gates

    A login, CAPTCHA or confirmation still stops the run and waits for you. A schedule does not buy its way past anything.

What it collects. Only what you script. Scheduled rule-based runs execute locally, so a morning check like this does not use your AI balance.
Limits worth respecting. Keep the frequency polite to the other side. A check every few minutes against a small vendor portal is how you get your own session rate-limited; once or twice a day is almost always enough.

The arithmetic, not a promise: multiply the minutes one batch costs you by hand against the 5 mornings a week you actually do. That product is what this replaces, for one sitting of about 4 min.
