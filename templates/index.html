How a template becomes yours

  1. Read the step list

    Every template states the targets to pick and the rules to add, in order, with what to check first.

  2. Build it on your own page

    Same picker, same rule engine — you are copying a recipe into the panel, not installing someone else’s script.

  3. Run one row, read the log

    The log is the review step. It shows which values came back empty before anything gets saved at volume.

  4. Save, reuse, schedule

    It becomes a normal ORCA workflow: yours to edit, keep locally, or put on the scheduler.
