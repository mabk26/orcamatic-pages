Your AI plan is a dollar balance, not a mystery credit system.

Routine saved workflows such as scheduled clicks, profile actions, or a form fill that does not need AI reasoning do not spend from your balance. The balance is used only when you choose an AI-assisted task that needs cloud reasoning.

Short tasks typically use a few cents. More involved, multi-step workflows use more because they require more reasoning. AI use is metered against your balance at a published per-token rate; no API key or separate provider account is needed.

If your AI plan lapses, your remaining balance is paused, not deleted: it is held for 7 days and restored in full when you renew. After 7 days it is forfeited. A failed card payment never pauses your balance — you have time to update the card.

A refunded AI subscription forfeits its remaining balance immediately.

When the balance runs out mid-month.

Nothing you built is held hostage. The automation you already scripted keeps running; only new AI-reasoned steps wait.

Keeps running

  • Saved workflows built from rules, waits and conditions
  • Scheduled runs on the times you set
  • Form filling and extraction that needs no reasoning step
  • Run logs and previously captured values

These are the majority of everyday automations, which is why most months the balance is never touched.

Waits for next month, or a top-up

  • Asking the visual planner to work out a new step
  • AI-assisted tasks that must interpret an unfamiliar page
  • Re-planning a workflow after a big interface change

Active AI plans receive a monthly balance. Cancel or change tier at any time through the Lemon Squeezy portal; new plans carry a 7-day money-back guarantee.

The exact remaining balance and per-run cost are shown in the panel, because a guess from a marketing page would be worse than no number at all.
