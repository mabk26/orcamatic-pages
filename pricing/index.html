Your plan is a dollar balance, not a mystery credit system.

Routine saved workflows such as scheduled clicks, profile actions, or a form fill that does not need AI reasoning do not spend from your balance. The balance is used only when you choose an AI-assisted task that needs cloud reasoning.

Short tasks typically use a few cents. More involved, multi-step workflows use more because they require more reasoning. The final cost is tied to the AI provider’s actual usage price rather than an arbitrary task count.
